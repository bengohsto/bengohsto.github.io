Teaching

Teaching philosophy

  • Every child can learn when the classroom is safe, caring and enabling.
  • Education serves democracy. Following John Dewey, schools should build democratic habits, including what Walter Parker calls “enlightened political engagement.”
  • Students come first. Learning should centre the student and grow into a community of learners.
  • Teaching is a shared endeavour, carried out with fellow teachers and in partnership with parents.
  • Pedagogy is a craft, and practice makes progress.

I want my students to form and defend their own views, to act with empathy, and to disagree respectfully.

Experience

Secondary school teaching, Singapore

I teach History and Social Studies at all levels in a secondary school in the south of Singapore. I have led the design of Lower Secondary History resources on Singapore, applied reading, writing and cognitive-science research to Upper Secondary History, and championed educational technology, including generative AI. I also led a Secondary 2 Historical Investigation on the school’s 70 years of contributions to Singapore.

Earlier placements: a 10-week internship (2016), a 5-week teaching attachment (2020), and a 10-week contract with a 4-week Teaching Assistantship (2023–2024).
